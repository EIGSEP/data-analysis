Measured: a roughly monotonic rise from 0.7% (07-13) to ~19.5% (07-17), not tracking file-count-per-day (07-13/07-14 are the longest days by file count and have the LOWEST new-outlier fractions, 07-17 is mid-sized and highest) and not simply mirroring v0's own RFI fraction, which is noisier and doesn't show the same clean trend. This means the DPSS-residual approach is catching something systematically more prevalent later in the campaign that v0's threshold-based detectors mostly don't.

Candidate explanation, not confirmed here: this session's own prior work flagged several time-localized self-RFI events concentrated later in the campaign window (07-16 1.25 MHz comb event, 07-17 suspected 15-min-interval LIDAR RFI) and a documented per-era escalation in self-generated interference. A rising DPSS-outlier fraction toward 07-16/07-17/07-18 is at least consistent with that timeline. This is a plausible read of the shape, not a demonstrated cause -- confirming it would need checking whether the new-outlier pixels concentrate in the specific frequency/time windows those known events occupy, not done here. Flagging as the single most important open question before treating this staged product as more than a first attempt.
